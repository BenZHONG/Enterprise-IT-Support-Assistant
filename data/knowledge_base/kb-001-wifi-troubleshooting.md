# Wi-Fi Troubleshooting

Company laptops use the EXAMPLE-CORP network. Visitors use EXAMPLE-GUEST.

## Check for an outage first

If many people in your office have the same problem, there may be a known
outage. Follow the Network Outage Procedure (KB-006) before troubleshooting.

## Steps for employees

1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again, and make sure airplane mode is off.
2. Choose "Forget" for EXAMPLE-CORP, then reconnect with your normal sign-in.
3. Restart your laptop.
4. Move closer to an access point or try another area of the office.

## When to raise a ticket

If the steps above do not work and there is no known outage, raise a ticket.
Include your office, what you tried, when the problem started, and any error
message. Never include your password.

## What IT Support checks

IT Support looks at the laptop's Wi-Fi adapter status, the last time the
laptop reported to device management, and outage records for your office.
A laptop that has not reported recently is not necessarily faulty. The
management agent may simply be switched off.
