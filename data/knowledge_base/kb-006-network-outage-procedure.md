# Network Outage Procedure for Employees

## Check the outage status

Before raising a ticket, check whether your office has an active outage for
the service that is failing (corporate Wi-Fi, VPN, or internet).

## If there is an active outage in your office

- Do not raise a ticket for the same symptoms. IT Support already knows.
- Follow the updates on the intranet. For a major outage, the first update
  is posted within 30 minutes and further updates are posted hourly.
- Ask your manager about working from another location if you must keep
  working.

## After the outage is resolved

Restart your laptop. If the problem continues, follow the guide for that
service (for example KB-001 for Wi-Fi) and raise a ticket if needed.

## If there is no active outage

Treat the problem as an individual fault and follow the guide for that
service.
