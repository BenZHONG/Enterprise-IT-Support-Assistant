# Hardware Faults and Replacements

## Quick checks

1. Restart the device.
2. Check power and cables, and try a different power point.
3. Write down any error message or light pattern.

## What needs a technician

- A Wi-Fi adapter that is reported as faulty or not detected.
- A battery with health below 60 percent.
- A screen, keyboard, or port that does not work after a restart.

Raise a ticket for these. Do not open the laptop case yourself.

## Loan laptops

Loan laptops are available at the Sydney and Melbourne offices for high
priority tickets. Other offices can arrange delivery through the service desk.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen device to the service desk immediately. Do not wait
until the end of the day.
