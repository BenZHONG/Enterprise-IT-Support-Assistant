# Microsoft 365 Problems

This guide covers Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, and the Office apps.

## Steps for employees

1. Sign out of the app and sign in again.
2. Try the web version at https://office.example.com in a browser.
3. Restart the app, then restart your laptop.
4. Check the service health page on the intranet at
   https://intranet.example.com/it/status.

## Common causes

- Repeated sign-in prompts are often caused by an expired session. Signing in
  again usually fixes it.
- A full mailbox stops new email arriving. Archive or delete old items.
- Teams call quality problems are often caused by a weak network connection.

## When to raise a ticket

Raise a ticket if the problem continues after the steps above and the service
health page shows no incident. Include the app name and the exact error text.
