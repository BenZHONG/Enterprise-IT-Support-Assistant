# Password Reset and Account Lockout

## Self-service reset

Use the self-service page at https://password.example.com to reset your
password. New passwords must be at least 14 characters long.

## Account lockout

After five failed sign-in attempts your account locks. It unlocks
automatically after 15 minutes. You can also use the self-service page.

## Security rules

- IT Support will never ask for your password or a multi-factor code.
- Never share your password, including in chat or in a ticket.
- If you think someone else knows your password, reset it immediately and
  tell the service desk.

## Multi-factor reset

If you lose your phone, contact the service desk. An IT Support Agent will
verify your identity with your manager before resetting multi-factor sign-in.
