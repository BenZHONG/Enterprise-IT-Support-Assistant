# Outage Escalation Runbook (IT Staff Only)

This runbook is for IT Support Agents and IT Administrators. Do not share
internal contact details with employees.

## When an outage is reported

1. Confirm there is an outage record for the region and service.
2. Link new tickets with the same symptoms to the outage record.
3. For a major outage, escalate to the Network Operations on-call team at
   network-ops@example.com.
4. Make sure the first employee update is posted within 30 minutes.

## After resolution

1. Ask affected employees to restart their devices.
2. Close linked tickets after 24 hours with no further reports.
3. Add a short summary to the outage record.
