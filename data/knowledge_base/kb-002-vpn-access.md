# VPN Access and Troubleshooting

You need the VPN only when working away from a company office. The VPN app
is called Example Secure Connect.

## Steps for employees

1. Confirm you have normal internet access, for example by opening a public
   website.
2. Close and reopen Example Secure Connect.
3. Sign in with your work account and approve the multi-factor prompt.
4. Restart your laptop and try again.
5. Try a different network, such as mobile data, if a hotel or cafe network
   blocks the VPN.

## Known outages

If your region has an active VPN outage, follow the Network Outage Procedure
(KB-006) instead of raising a new ticket.

## When to raise a ticket

Raise a ticket if the VPN still fails on two different networks. Include the
exact error text and the time of your last attempt.
